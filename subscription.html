Billing Status

Status and Renewal

Future status rows will use the verified billing projection for Auto-renew, Renewal or expiration timing, cancellation effective dates, grace windows, and recovery actions.

  • Current plan and access state come from the parent billing projection.
  • Renewal or expiration countdowns use verified projection dates.
  • Free practice remains available while billing status refreshes.
Payment History

Receipts and Invoices

Future payment history rows will show app-owned dates, amounts, statuses, plans, masked payment method labels, receipt or invoice links, and refund or dispute status without provider details.

  • Receipt and invoice links stay on app billing routes.
  • Failed-payment recovery actions remain server mediated.
  • Full card numbers, wallet identifiers, provider payloads, learner IDs, and student names are never shown.
Billing Management

Manage Safely

Future management actions will be explicit server-mediated requests for payment method updates, Cancel at period end, reactivation, and Renew one-time access.

  • One-time renewal stays separate from recurring subscription signup.
  • Converting one-time access to auto-renew requires explicit confirmation.
  • Browser actions cannot directly change provider records or paid entitlement.
Billing Recovery States

Clear Recovery Copy

Billing recovery states will explain no subscription, checkout pending, payment processing, failed payment, provider unavailable, webhook delay, renewal failure, expiration, refund, dispute, and canceled-at-period-end cases.

  • Free practice and parent dashboard access remain predictable while billing refreshes.
  • Recovery actions are server mediated and non-destructive.
  • Messages avoid blame, pressure, provider internals, and paid-access overpromises.

One-time

Checkout Readiness

Payment Method Availability

Checkout launch will stay server-owned and will only show methods supported for the selected plan, browser, device, region, and renewal type.

  • Major cards use hosted checkout or provider elements.
  • Apple Pay appears only when the browser, device, region, and provider support it.
  • PayPal remains a wallet option when it is supported for the selected plan.
  • Venmo can fall back to another supported method without changing the selected plan.
